# Aurora: 4 hook scripts for talking-head Meta ads

Based on the "4 Meta Ads that convert" reference reel (Transformation, Promise, Negative, Visual hooks).
Each script runs 15–25s. One person speaks to camera at chest height, with an Aurora lit on the wall behind or beside them.

**Claims check (v4 handoff rules).** Keep to these facts:
- solid teak; 3000K warm light; light goes up and down; hidden bracket; sizes 30″ and 48″; from ₹1,999
- "Made by hand in Khatauli"
- exactly "Free shipping on prepaid orders"

Do **not** promise delivery or dispatch times. Do **not** name or attack another brand.

**On-screen style (copy the reference):**
- top-left heading bar
- the hook line in a white card for the first 2 seconds
- captions of 1–3 words in the lower third, kept above y≈1450 so Instagram's UI doesn't cover them

---

## 1. Transformation hook
**Heading bar:** RAW TEAK → WALL LIGHT · **Hook card:** "We turned THIS → into THIS"

| Time | Say | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | "We turned **this**… into **this**." | Hold up a raw teak plank, then point to the lit Aurora on the wall. |
| 2–8s | "Every Aurora starts as solid teak. We cut it, plane it, route a channel for the light, and sand it by hand." | Cut to the workshop B-roll (already in the edited ad). |
| 8–11s | "Then we switch it on." | The light switches on. Hold 2 beats. |
| 11–18s | "Soft 3000K warm light, up **and** down the wall. Made by hand in Khatauli. From ₹1,999." | Medium shot next to the light, hand gesturing along it. |
| 18–20s | "Link's below. Free shipping on prepaid orders." | Point down. |

## 2. Promise hook
**Heading bar:** PLAIN WALL? · **Hook card:** "We can turn a plain wall into the warmest corner of your home"

| Time | Say | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | "We can turn a plain wall into the warmest corner of your home, so you come home to soft light, not a harsh tube light." | Start in front of a bare wall. Step aside to reveal the Aurora switched on. |
| 3–10s | "It's one bar of solid teak. Light goes up **and** down the wall. The bracket's hidden, so it looks like it's floating." | Close-up of the grain, the glow above and below, then the side gap. |
| 10–15s | "Comes in 30 inch and 48 inch. From ₹1,999." | Arm along the bar for scale. |
| 15–18s | "Free shipping on prepaid orders. Tap the link." | Point down. |

## 3. Negative hook
**Heading bar:** BEFORE YOU BUY · **Hook card:** "Don't buy a wall light online… until you check these 3 things"

| Time | Say | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | "Don't buy a wall light online until you check these three things." | Hold up 3 fingers. |
| 3–7s | "One: is it actually solid wood? Look for real grain like this." | Close-up of the teak grain. |
| 7–11s | "Two: is the light **warm**? 3000K is the cosy, golden kind, not the blue-white kind." | The glow on the wall. |
| 11–15s | "Three: does it light up **and** down? That's what makes a wall glow." | Up and down beams. |
| 15–20s | "That's why we built the Aurora. Solid teak, made by hand in Khatauli. From ₹1,999, free shipping on prepaid orders." | Full shot plus the presenter. |

*Note:* this script stays positive about the Aurora and never names or criticises other sellers.

## 4. Visual hook
**Heading bar:** YOUR WALL · **Hook card:** "Does your wall look like THIS, THIS or THIS?"

| Time | Say | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | "Does your wall look like **this**… **this**… or **this**?" | Point to three panels that pop up beside you: a bare wall, a harsh tube light, a dark corner at night. **These three shots need filming; we don't have them yet.** |
| 4–6s | "…but you want it to look like **THIS**?" | Big "THIS" card. Cut to the Aurora glowing on the wall. |
| 6–14s | "Here's the fix: one bar of solid teak with a warm 3000K glow, up and down the wall. 30 or 48 inch." | Grain close-up, the glow, arm for scale. |
| 14–18s | "From ₹1,999. Free shipping on prepaid orders. Link below." | Point down. |

## Filming checklist
- Film vertically (9:16). Shoot the "after" wall at dusk or with the room lights off so the glow shows.
- Before shooting, lower the camera's shutter or exposure and check the LED doesn't flicker on screen. Several workshop clips strobed.
- Get one clean, steady **vertical full shot** of the whole Aurora lit, from far enough back that nothing sits below it.
- For #4, film a bare wall, a tube-lit wall and a dark corner, 3 seconds each.

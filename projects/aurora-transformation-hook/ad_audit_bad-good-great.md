# Aurora ads against the "Meta ads. Fast." Bad / Good / Great framework

Reference: Christian Jamal reel (hook / offer / proof / button / creative / targeting).
This grades the Aurora ads made so far and gives the "Great" version of each part.
Anything marked **OWNER** needs a real fact from you before it can go in an ad. Nothing here is invented.

| Part | Where we are now | Grade | "Great" for the Aurora | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | "We turned THIS into THIS" (process); variants: tube-light upgrade, stop buying, what ₹1,999 gets you, what's inside | Good → Great | Name the *moment* the buyer feels, not the product: "Your living room looks like an office at night?" or "Guests always notice the lights first." | Nothing; can be filmed or text-cut now |
| **Offer** | "from ₹1,999 · Free shipping on prepaid orders" | Good | Risk reversal, like "Don't love it? Send it back within X days." | **OWNER**: your actual return/replacement policy. Only use it if it's real. |
| **Proof** | None in the ads yet | Bad (missing) | "[N] homes lit this year. Here's one of them." Then a real customer's wall, photo or video, with permission. | **OWNER**: a real order count you're happy to publish, plus 1–2 customer photos or videos you have permission to use |
| **Button** | "SHOP NOW" | Good | Make the next step feel tiny: "Pick 30″ or 48″. Checkout takes a minute." Keep Meta's button set to *Shop now*. | Nothing (but check the checkout really is that quick on mobile) |
| **Creative** | Real workshop and product footage, text-led; no person speaking | Good | "You, on your phone, talking to the camera." The founder or maker in Khatauli, holding the teak, switching the light on. Use `hook_scripts.md`. | A 20-minute phone shoot |
| **Targeting** | (not set by us) | – | Broad targeting (India, age range only) and let the creative find buyers; test the hook variants against each other | Set this in Ads Manager |

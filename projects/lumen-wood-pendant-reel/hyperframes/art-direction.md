# Art direction — "One Line of Light"

**Design read:** quiet Japandi luxury. The product IS a line of light, so the film is built as one
sentence spoken across the cuts, and one drawn line.

- Palette: deep walnut-black `#120D09`, walnut `#5A3D28`, amber glow `#F3C98B`, warm cream `#F4EBDD`.
- Grade: footage pulled from cool phone-blue to ~2900K amber, lowered exposure so the LED reads as the
  brightest thing in frame, soft vignette.
- Type: Cormorant Garamond italic (display, the "voice") + Manrope tracked small caps (labels).
- Signature device: a 2px amber vertical line that *draws itself* — used in exactly two beats
  (hook open, end card). Scarce on purpose.
- Motion: slow-in/slow-out pushes on footage (contemplative), type rises 24px with blur-to-sharp
  (light "coming into focus"); the only fast passage is the 4-cut beat run before the reveal.
- Transitions: hard cuts on the beat; one light-bloom (white-amber flash) into the end card.
- Anti-patterns: no stock templates, no bouncy easing, no more than ~5 words on screen at once.
